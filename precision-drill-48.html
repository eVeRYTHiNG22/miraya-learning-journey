Precision Drill 48
Read it. Turn it. Finish it.

Thirteen one-mark Chemistry questions, all inside your SA-1 syllabus. The content is deliberately low-difficulty — everything here is work you have already done. What is being tested is the habit: read the demand exactly, turn the ratio the right way up, and take the last step.

Before you begin

Time allowed

Subject

Marking
Fully automatic
  • Answer all 13 questions. Each is worth exactly 1 mark. Q1 is multiple choice; the other twelve each want a number typed into the box.
  • Every question is Chemistry, but the topic keeps changing as you go — states of matter, moles and concentration, redox, petrochemicals, reactivity of metals, separation techniques and organic chemistry. Read each one from scratch; that switch is part of the exercise.
  • Marking is all-or-nothing and automatic. There is no method mark and nothing to tick yourself, so an answer that is right except for the direction of a fraction or the last keystroke scores zero — exactly as a wrong answer would.
  • Where a question prints an instruction (“to the nearest 10 g”, “for how many minutes”, “the percentage by mass”), that instruction is the question. Q10’s rounding instruction is marked exactly; elsewhere a correctly-worked value is accepted whether or not you rounded it.
  • On Q2–Q13, type just the number — the unit is already printed beside the box. Write it as an ordinary decimal (0.05), not in standard form.
  • In Timed exam mode a 15-minute countdown runs; the drill locks and marks itself when you submit or time runs out.
  • After marking, every question opens up a worked answer that names the specific slip the question was built to catch. Read those even for the ones you got right.
