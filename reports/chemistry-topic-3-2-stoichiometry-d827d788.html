⚠ The one thing costing you marks: rearranging the concentration formula

Good news: in (b) and (d) you carried your numbers through correctly, so you still earned those marks (error carried forward). Lock the formula down with the triangle:
   ┌───────┐
   │   n   │    n = amount (mol)
   ├───┬───┤    c = concentration (mol/dm³)
   │ c │ V │    V = volume (dm³)
   └───┴───┘
  Cover what you want →   n = c×V  ·  c = n÷V  ·  V = n÷c
  And always convert cm³ → dm³ first:   ÷ 1000
