Exam conditions — practise the way you will sit it

  • No calculator, no log tables, no rough paper. Work in the margin of a printout, or on the booklet the way you will on the day.
  • Every question has exactly one correct option, labelled (1) to (4).
  • A wrong answer costs you a mark; a blank costs nothing but earns nothing. Even a blind guess across four options is worth +0.25 on average, and +0.67 once you rule one out — so never leave a blank. See the strategy page.
  • About 90 seconds per question. The bar at the top shows how long you have been on the question you are looking at.
  • Use 🚩 Flag to mark a question to come back to. Anything still flagged and blank is listed for you at the end.

Practice mode still marks you and still applies −1 — only the countdown is off. “Just look” shows the questions without marking or recording anything.
