How weightage works. Weightage is how much each assessment counts toward the subject grade — e.g. two Formatives at 10% each and a Summative at 30% within a term. The subject average is weighted by these numbers; the note under each subject tells you whether they add up to 100%.
Approx. grade maps your percentage to the Cambridge 9–1 scale as a rough guide only — real grade boundaries are set for each exam session and differ by subject and paper.
